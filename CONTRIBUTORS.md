# Contributors

The book is written by Soufian Zaouam. The people below have translated, reviewed or corrected it; the editions are listed with the names of those who contributed to them, in the order of their first merged contribution. Add yourself in the pull request that carries your first contribution (name or GitHub username, as you prefer, with the chapters or the kind of work). Release notes credit the contributions of each version.

Contributions are made under the terms of [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#before-you-start-the-licence): every edition remains under the book's licence, CC BY-NC-ND 4.0, with the author as licensor.
